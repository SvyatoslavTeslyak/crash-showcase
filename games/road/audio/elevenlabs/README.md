# Goat Road scene effects

Generated with ElevenLabs `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`. Provenance and processing are in `sources.json`.

- `hop_0.wav`–`hop_3.wav`: universal jump (springy whoosh and soft landing tap, no animal voice) for every character; takes rotate in order.
- `hoof.ogg`: quiet hoof pair for the safe arrival.
- `hit.ogg`: compact vehicle bump.
- `collapse.ogg`: wooden roof failure.
- `splash.ogg`: water fall.
- `squash_dog.ogg`, `squash_chicken.ogg`, `squash_goat.ogg`: soft toy squash and a tiny animal reaction on vehicle contact. All three goat variants share the goat clip. Replaces the generic impact for these characters, with no stacked extra hit sound.

Mono 24 kHz Vorbis, short clips with faded edges. Original takes remain outside the project. Small pitch variation avoids extra files. UI controls, cashout readiness, Win and wallet transfer belong to the shared Crash UI kit.
